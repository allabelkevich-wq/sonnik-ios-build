Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 · Data controller: Yupland Digital Solutions, LLC

Lunar Dream Journal turns a dream you describe into a written reading, using the lunar day and, if you choose to add them, your birth details. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is kept, and how you delete it.

The app is operated by Yupland Digital Solutions, LLC, registered in Georgia. Where this policy says «we», it means that company.

1. What we collect

  • Account identifier. When you sign in with Apple we receive a unique identifier for your account. We do not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • Your dreams. The text of each dream you record and the reading generated for it. Together they make up your dream journal and your monthly summaries.
  • Questions to the dream oracle and the answers to them.
  • Voice. If you dictate a dream, the recording is sent to be converted into text. We keep the text; we do not store the recording itself.
  • Birth details, if you add them — date, and optionally time and place of birth — for the personal forecast. You can use the app without them.
  • Time zone, so the lunar day is calculated for where you are.
  • Purchases. Payment happens inside Apple's system. We receive a confirmation that a purchase succeeded, its product and transaction identifiers, price and currency. We never see your card details.
  • Technical data — IP address, device language, and anonymous records of which screens were opened — used to run the service, fix errors and prevent abuse.

2. Why we use it

  • Generating readings, oracle answers, the personal forecast and monthly summaries
  • Keeping your dream journal available on every device where you sign in
  • Confirming purchases and applying your access
  • Answering you when you write to support
  • Preventing abuse of free readings

3. Who else processes it

We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising. To make the app work, a small set of processors receive only what they need:

  • Apple — sign-in and payment
  • RevenueCat — confirming that a purchase is genuine
  • DeepSeek — generating the text of readings and oracle answers
  • Groq — converting dictated dreams into text

DeepSeek receives the text of your dream or question, the lunar data and, if you added them, your birth details. It does not receive your account identifier.

The app carries no advertising or analytics SDKs, and nothing in it follows you across other apps or websites. That is why the app never asks for tracking permission.

4. Where your data is stored

Our database is located in Moscow, Russia. Because the app is available worldwide, your data is transferred to and processed in Russia regardless of where you live. If that is not acceptable to you, please do not use the app.

Connections are encrypted in transit. Access to the database is limited to the people who operate the service.

5. How long we keep it

Your dream journal, readings and profile are kept while your account exists.

6. Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Profile and choose Delete account. This deletes your dreams together with their readings and your monthly summaries, and erases your name, birth details and natal chart from your profile. It cannot be undone. Records of purchases are kept, as accounting requires.

You may also write to us and we will delete the account for you.

7. Children

Lunar Dream Journal is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will remove it.

8. If you live in California

Under the CCPA and CPRA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask how it was used. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of. Exercising any of these rights will never degrade the service you receive. Write to the address below and we will answer within 45 days.

9. If you live in the European Economic Area or the UK

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and you may object to processing. The legal basis for generating your readings is the contract you enter when you use the app; for preventing abuse it is our legitimate interest. As explained in section 4, your data is processed in Russia, which has not received an adequacy decision.

10. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
